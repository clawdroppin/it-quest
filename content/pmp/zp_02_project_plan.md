@@lesson xp-project_plan Plan a Real IT Project
@@text
Pick a real or realistic IT project (a Microsoft 365 migration, a new office network, a website relaunch) and produce the core planning documents as a project manager would: a one-page charter, a stakeholder register with the power/interest grid, a WBS down to work packages, a schedule run through this track's critical path calculator, a risk register with responses, and a communication plan.

**You'll practise:** project charter · WBS · stakeholder analysis · risk register · communication planning

## Milestones

1. **Charter**: purpose, objectives (SMART), scope in and out, milestones, budget, sponsor.
2. **Stakeholders**: at least eight, placed on the power/interest grid with an engagement plan.
3. **WBS**: deliverables broken into work packages of 8–80 hours.
4. **Schedule**: activities and dependencies in project.json, run through the Office Move calculator.
5. **Risks and communication**: ten risks with owners and responses; who gets which report how often.

## You're done when

- A colleague could start the project from your documents, and the AI helper's review found no major gaps.

## Getting help

Open the project, then the 🤖 **AI Helper**: it sees the lesson and your files. Good questions to start with:

- *"Review my charter's objectives: are they SMART?"*
- *"What risks am I missing for a Microsoft 365 migration?"*

Ask for hints, reviews and explanations rather than finished code: you learn by writing it.
