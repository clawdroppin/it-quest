@@text
Renting an apartment in Japan costs much more up front than in most countries, and the listing abbreviations hide a lot. A ¥90,000-a-month flat can easily need ¥450,000 or more on the day you sign. This lesson explains how to read a listing, what each initial cost is, the two kinds of lease, what renewal costs, and what you will and won't have to pay for when you move out.

## Reading a listing (物件情報)

- **間取り** (layout):
  - **R** is one room with the kitchen in it.
  - **K** is a separate kitchen.
  - **DK** is a dining-kitchen.
  - **LDK** is a living-dining-kitchen.
  - The number in front counts the other rooms: **1K** is one room plus a kitchen, and **2LDK** is two rooms plus an LDK.
- **専有面積** (floor area) is given in ㎡. Rooms are often described in **畳** (jō, tatami mats): by industry rules 1畳 is at least 1.62 ㎡, so a 6畳 room is about 10 ㎡.
- **家賃** (rent) is often written in 万円: 9.8万円 = ¥98,000. Add **管理費** or **共益費** (management or common-area fee), usually ¥3,000–¥15,000 a month.
- **敷1 礼1** means a deposit (敷金) of one month's rent and key money (礼金) of one month. **敷0 礼0** (ゼロゼロ) is cheaper up front, but check the cleaning fee and the contract type.
- **駅徒歩8分** means an 8-minute walk from the station, calculated at 80 m per minute in a straight-ish line. Real walks are often longer.
- **築年数** (building age): buildings from June 1981 onward meet the newer earthquake code (新耐震基準), and a 2000 revision strengthened it further for wooden houses. **構造**: RC or SRC (reinforced concrete) is quieter and more solid than 木造 (wooden) or 軽量鉄骨 (light steel).

## Initial costs (初期費用)

| Item | Typical amount | Returned? |
|---|---|---|
| 敷金 (deposit) | 0–2 months' rent | Mostly, minus repairs beyond normal wear |
| 礼金 (key money) | 0–2 months | No |
| 仲介手数料 (agent fee) | Up to 1 month + 10% tax (often 0.5 month) | No |
| 前家賃 (first month's rent, plus the pro-rated rest of the move-in month) | 1–2 months | No |
| 保証会社 (guarantor company) | 50–100% of one month's rent + fees, then about ¥10,000 a year | No |
| 火災保険 (fire insurance) | About ¥15,000–20,000 for two years | No |
| 鍵交換 (lock change) | ¥15,000–25,000 | No |
| Cleaning fee (クリーニング代), sometimes charged at move-in | ¥30,000–50,000 | No |

A rule of thumb: budget **4.5–6 months' rent**. Ask whether your employer pays part of it, and whether they offer **住宅手当** (housing allowance) or a **社宅** (company-leased flat, where you pay a fraction of the rent).

## Contract types and renewal

- **普通借家契約** (standard lease): usually 2 years and renewed automatically. The landlord needs a **legitimate reason** (正当事由) to refuse renewal, so tenants are well protected.
- **定期借家契約** (fixed-term lease): ends at the agreed date. Renewal is a new contract that the landlord can refuse. It's often cheaper, but you may have to leave.
- **更新料** (renewal fee): typically one month's rent every 2 years in the Tokyo area (it's rare in some regions). Add it to your long-term cost comparison, along with the guarantor company's renewal fee.
- **Notice to move out**: usually **1 month** (sometimes 2), in writing or on the agent's form. Rent may be charged per day or for the whole final month: check.
- **Early termination penalty** (短期解約違約金): common with ゼロゼロ flats, e.g. one month's rent if you leave within a year.

## Moving out (退去) and 原状回復

- **原状回復** (restoration to the original condition): you pay for damage you caused (a hole in the wall, cigarette burns, pet damage, stains from neglect). The landlord pays for **normal wear and tear** (経年劣化 and 通常損耗): sun-faded wallpaper, marks from furniture, an ageing tatami. The Ministry of Land (国土交通省) publishes guidelines on this, so cite them if a deduction looks unfair.
- Take dated photos of everything when you move in, and attend the move-out inspection (立会い).
- Admin when moving:
  - file a **転出届** (moving-out notice) at your old ward office before moving, and a **転入届** at the new one within **14 days**.
  - update the address on your residence card at the same time.
  - tell your bank, employer and phone company, and set up mail forwarding (転居届) with Japan Post.

import math
import re

listings = [
    "1LDK 35.2㎡ 家賃9.8万円 管理費8000円 敷1 礼1 徒歩8分",
    "1K 22.5㎡ 家賃7.2万円 管理費5000円 敷0 礼0 徒歩12分",
    "2DK 40.1㎡ 家賃9.5万円 管理費3000円 敷1 礼0 徒歩15分",
    "1R 18.0㎡ 家賃6.4万円 管理費6000円 敷1 礼1 徒歩3分",
]

def parse(s):
    rent = round(float(re.search(r"家賃([\d.]+)万円", s).group(1)) * 10000)
    fee = int(re.search(r"管理費(\d+)円", s).group(1))
    return {
        "layout": s.split()[0],
        "area": float(re.search(r"([\d.]+)㎡", s).group(1)),
        "rent": rent,
        "fee": fee,
        "monthly": rent + fee,
        "shiki": int(re.search(r"敷(\d)", s).group(1)),
        "rei": int(re.search(r"礼(\d)", s).group(1)),
        "walk": int(re.search(r"徒歩(\d+)分", s).group(1)),
    }

rows = [parse(s) for s in listings]
for r in sorted(rows, key=lambda r: r["monthly"]):
    print(f"{r['layout']:<5} {r['area']:>5.1f}㎡ ({r['area'] / 1.62:4.1f}畳)  ¥{r['monthly']:>7,}/month  "
          f"¥{r['rent'] / r['area']:>5,.0f}/㎡  敷{r['shiki']} 礼{r['rei']}  walk ~{math.ceil(r['walk'] * 1.2)} min")

import calendar
from datetime import date

flats = {
    "Flat A (敷1 礼1)": {"rent": 98000, "fee": 8000, "shiki": 1, "rei": 1, "agent": 1.0, "guarantor": 0.5, "zero_penalty": False},
    "Flat B (ゼロゼロ)": {"rent": 92000, "fee": 10000, "shiki": 0, "rei": 0, "agent": 0.5, "guarantor": 1.0, "zero_penalty": True},
}
MOVE_IN = date(2026, 4, 10)
FIRE_INSURANCE, LOCK, CLEANING = 18000, 22000, 40000
GUARANTOR_YEARLY, RENEWAL_MONTHS = 10000, 1
DEPOSIT_BACK = 0.7

def initial_costs(f, move_in):
    monthly = f["rent"] + f["fee"]
    days = calendar.monthrange(move_in.year, move_in.month)[1]
    return {
        "敷金 deposit": f["rent"] * f["shiki"],
        "礼金 key money": f["rent"] * f["rei"],
        "仲介手数料 agent fee": round(f["rent"] * f["agent"] * 1.1),
        "日割り家賃 pro-rated rent": round(monthly * (days - move_in.day + 1) / days),
        "前家賃 next month": monthly,
        "保証会社 guarantor": round(monthly * f["guarantor"]),
        "火災保険 fire insurance": FIRE_INSURANCE,
        "鍵交換 lock change": LOCK,
        "クリーニング cleaning": CLEANING if f["zero_penalty"] else 0,
    }

def two_year_total(f, move_in):
    initial = sum(initial_costs(f, move_in).values())
    later = 22 * (f["rent"] + f["fee"])
    renewal = RENEWAL_MONTHS * f["rent"] + GUARANTOR_YEARLY
    refund = round(DEPOSIT_BACK * f["rent"] * f["shiki"])
    return initial + later + renewal - refund

for name, f in flats.items():
    items = initial_costs(f, MOVE_IN)
    total = sum(items.values())
    print(f"{name}: initial ¥{total:,} ({total / f['rent']:.1f} months' rent)")
    for item, amount in items.items():
        if amount:
            print(f"    {item:<26} ¥{amount:>8,}")
    print(f"  two-year total ¥{two_year_total(f, MOVE_IN):,}")
